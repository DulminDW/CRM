## 2. Key Synthesis Points

1. **Single Adjustment:** Ranging between **-$5 and -$15** (never zero, never percentage).
2. **Triple Adjustment:** Ranging between **+\$10 and +\$55** depending on whether third bed is quoted room-only or covers 3 full meals.
3. **Double BB Reduction:** Ranging between **-$5 and -$15** (predominantly -$10).
4. **Double FB Supplement:** Ranging between **+\$14 and +\$50** (predominantly +\$15 to +\$20).
5. **No Universal Linear Formula:** Imposing a single formula like `Single = Double - 10` would incorrectly price 7 out of 12 hotels.
