# RATE TEMPLATE & CALCULATION SETUP GUIDE
**Window Companies Management System — User Operation Manual**  
**Document Code:** `RATE_TEMPLATE_CONFIGURATION_GUIDE.md`  
**Date:** November 05, 2026  

---

## 1. Quick Start Workflow

1. Navigate to **Rate Calculator** in the sidebar.
2. Select target **Hotel** and contract **Season**.
3. Choose the appropriate **Rate Tier**:
   - `RED` (Lowest Rate / Volume Tier)
   - `GREEN` (Normal Rate / Standard Tier)
   - `YELLOW` (Highest Rate / Ceiling Tier)
4. (Optional) Choose a previously saved setup from the **Reusable Setups** dropdown to prefill your standard adjustment rules.
5. In the **Primary Anchor Input** card, enter the base price for **Double HB**.
6. The entire 12-cell matrix immediately computes in real time!

---

## 2. Overriding a Price Manually

1. Click on any cell in the table (e.g., *Triple FB*).
2. Enter the negotiated or contractual custom figure in the prompt.
3. Click the checkmark to confirm.
4. The cell updates with an **OVERRIDE** badge in amber and records an audit log.
5. To undo an override, click the **Auto** button beneath the cell.

---

## 3. Saving a Reusable Setup (Optional)

1. Click **Save as Setup (Optional)** in the top right.
2. Enter a descriptive name (e.g., *Kandy Boutique HB Setup*, *India Market Promotional*).
3. Click **Save Setup**.
4. The setup is now saved to your reusable list and can be applied to any hotel at any time.

---

## 4. Saving the Final Rate Sheet

1. Once the rate matrix is finalized, click **Save Rate Sheet**.
2. Confirm the property, season, and tier.
3. The snapshot is stored immutably with full audit traceability.
