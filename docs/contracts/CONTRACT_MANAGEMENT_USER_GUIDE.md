# Contract Management User Guide
**Window Companies (Pvt) Ltd. — Operations & Sales Handbook**

---

## 1. Navigating to Contracts
1. Open the sidebar navigation.
2. Under **Hotels & Pricing**, select **Contracts**.
3. The page displays the master registry of all hotel agreements with real-time status badges (`Active`, `Draft`, `Expired`, `Terminated`).

---

## 2. Registering a New Partner Contract
1. Click **+ New Contract** in the top right.
2. **Select Hotel**: Choose the property (e.g. Jetwing Vil Uyana, Heritance Kandalama).
3. **Contract Reference**: Provide a unique reference code (e.g. `CTR-VIL-2026-001`).
4. **Contract Name**: E.g., `Annual FIT & Group Commercial Agreement 2026/2027`.
5. **Validity Period**: Specify `Valid From` and `Valid To`.
6. **Currency**: Select agreement billing currency (USD, EUR, GBP, LKR).
7. **Applicable Seasons**: Link one or more configured seasons for that property.
8. **Commercial Terms & Conditions**: Paste or type the agreed clauses.
9. **Internal Confidential Notes**: Enter internal-only information (negotiated margins, internal contact numbers). This is protected by the internal privacy shield and will never be shared with clients.
10. Click **Register Master Contract**.

---

## 3. Inspecting Contract Details, Conditions & Documents
- Click **Inspect** or the **Eye icon** on any contract row.
- The contract drawer will open with 5 tabs:
  - **Overview**: Property, reference, currency, lifecycle status, validity dates.
  - **Conditions**: Categorized rules (Cancellation, Check-in/out, Child Policy, Payment, Supplements). Internal conditions are highlighted with a padlock badge.
  - **Versions**: Version history (`v1.0`, `v2.0`) with timestamp and change logs.
  - **Documents**: Uploaded PDFs and attachments.
  - **Confidential Notes**: Internal notes accessible only to authorized staff.

---

## 4. Publishing a New Contract Version
When a contract is renegotiated or terms are amended:
1. Open the contract details drawer.
2. Navigate to the **Versions** tab.
3. Click **+ Publish New Version**.
4. Enter the new version label (e.g. `v2.0 (Mid-Season Amendment)`), effective dates, and a change summary.
5. Click **Publish Version Snapshot**.
6. The contract version increments, and the historical snapshot remains permanently preserved.

---

## 5. Using Contracts in Outward Rate Proposals
1. Navigate to **Share Rates** (`/share-rates`).
2. When you select a **Hotel** and a **Season**, the system automatically filters and displays eligible, active contracts.
3. Inspecting the proposal email preview will show:
   - The bound contract reference and version (e.g. `CTR-VIL-2026-001 (v1.0)`).
   - Only public commercial conditions.
   - Any internal notes remain strictly excluded.
4. Saving the quote creates an immutable snapshot linking the exact contract version used.
